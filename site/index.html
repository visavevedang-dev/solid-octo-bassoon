Questions photographers actually ask

Is my booking data backed up anywhere?+

Yes — your pipeline and booking data sync automatically to the cloud, so reinstalling the app or switching devices doesn't mean starting over.

What happens to a gallery if my subscription lapses?+

The gallery is deleted as soon as the subscription lapses, so renew before that happens if you want to keep it accessible to your client.

Is Slancio available on iOS?+

Yes — Slancio is available on both Android and iOS.

Do I need to migrate my existing bookings manually?+

Yes, for now — there's no bulk import from spreadsheets or other CRMs. Most photographers start fresh with new inquiries as they come in.
