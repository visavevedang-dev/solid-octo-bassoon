Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

Slancio is a business management app for independent wedding and event photographers. This page explains what we collect, why, and how it's handled — for both photographers using the app and their clients, who interact with Slancio only through shared links and never create an account.

Who this covers

  • Photographers — the app's account holders, identified and authenticated through our identity provider (Clerk).
  • Clients — couples and event clients who use a shared chat link or gallery link. Clients never sign up, log in, or install anything; access is scoped to a single unguessable link tied to their booking.

What we collect

  • Account & profile data — business name, contact info, and authentication details, managed through Clerk.
  • Booking data — couple names, venue, dates, and budget notes you enter or that are extracted from pasted inquiry text. This currently lives on your device; it is not yet synced to a cloud backup (see the note below).
  • Chat messages — messages exchanged between photographer and client through a booking's chat thread, capped at 5,000 characters per message.
  • Gallery photos — images you upload for a booking, stored on our object storage provider (Cloudflare R2) and shared with your client via a private link or QR code.
  • Identity verification data — if you opt into the Verified Badge, your ID document and a liveness selfie are processed by our verification provider (Didit) during that flow. We store only a one-way hash of your identity reference and the resulting verification status — never the raw document or biometric data.
  • Phone verification data — where phone verification is used, we store a one-way hash of the phone number, not the number itself.
  • Subscription & billing data — your plan tier and subscription status, synced from Google Play Billing via RevenueCat. We do not process or store raw payment card details ourselves.
Current limitation, stated plainly: booking and pipeline data is stored locally on your device today and is not yet backed up to the cloud. If you lose or reset your device before this ships, that data cannot be recovered by us. Cloud backup is a near-term roadmap priority.

How we use it

  • To operate the booking pipeline, client chat, and gallery delivery features you use directly.
  • To enforce your plan's active-booking limits and gallery storage quota.
  • To display your Verified Badge status on your public Discover profile, if you've completed verification.
  • To keep a delivered gallery accessible to your client for 30 days after a subscription lapses, before it is permanently deleted.

Who we share it with

We use a small set of specialist providers to run the app, and share only what each one needs to do its job:

  • Clerk — authentication and identity.
  • Cloudflare R2 — gallery photo storage, uploaded directly from your device via a short-lived, single-use upload link.
  • Didit — identity verification, only if you start the Verified Badge flow.
  • RevenueCat & Google Play Billing — subscription management and payment processing.

We do not sell your data or your clients' data to third parties.

Your client's data

A client's chat messages and gallery access are scoped to a single private link tied to their booking. Either party can block a chat thread, which stops new messages without deleting history. Clients don't create an account with us and have no separate login to manage.

Data retention & deletion

  • Deleting your account removes your chat threads, messages, attachments, and identity-verification records.
  • Payment records are kept separately for as long as required by tax and legal obligations, even after account deletion.
  • Gallery photos are deleted when a gallery is removed manually, or automatically 30 days after a subscription lapses.

Security

Access to bookings, chat, and galleries is tied to your authenticated identity — never to a client-supplied or request-supplied value. Identity and phone references are stored as one-way hashes rather than raw values. Administrative and webhook checks use constant-time comparison to prevent timing-based attacks.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or a request to delete your data can be sent to support@framiostudio.online.
